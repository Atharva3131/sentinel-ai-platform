# Autonomous AI Operations Platform

Version: 1.0

---

## Vision

Build a production-grade AI runtime platform capable of executing autonomous agent workflows with reliability, observability, evaluation, and self-healing.

The platform itself is generic.

The flagship demonstration is an AI SRE capable of detecting, diagnosing, and recovering production incidents.

---

## Problem

Current AI agents are capable of reasoning but lack production-grade operational capabilities.

Most systems cannot

- recover automatically
- evaluate themselves
- explain decisions
- trace executions
- audit workflows
- survive failures
- coordinate distributed execution

The project addresses this gap.

---

## Objectives

Build an event-driven runtime.

Support distributed agent orchestration.

Provide GraphRAG.

Provide automatic recovery.

Provide observability.

Provide evaluation.

Provide policy enforcement.

Provide human approval.

Provide auditability.

---

## Non Goals

Not another chatbot.

Not a frontend-heavy application.

Not a prompt engineering project.

Not model fine-tuning.

---
